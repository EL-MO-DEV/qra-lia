# Video script — 90 seconds (FR + EN subtitles are mandatory)

Record with **Loom** (screen + optional camera) or a phone screen recording. Keep it ≤ 90 s.
Never show keys, the Vercel dashboard, or real personal data (cover names/CIN/RIB on the paper).
Use a real person (with consent) if possible; never present AI-generated people as real users.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over (Darija) | Subtitle FR / EN |
|---|---|---|---|

Tips:
- Record the **voice step when the TTS quota is fresh** (wait 1 min after other tests), or on an iPhone (good fallback Arabic voice).
- If the live AI is slow during recording, cut the waiting time in editing — don't fake results; `?mock=` results show a DEMO banner.
- Export, upload, open the link **logged out** before pasting it in the form.
